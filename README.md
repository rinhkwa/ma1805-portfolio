
Drawing
I used a standard iPhone cat emoji as a basis for my drawing and tried to replicate it using standard commands for drawing various shapes.


Movement
For the ‘movement’ theme, my original plan was to write the code where a cat endlessly chases a mouse, but due to my current skill level I gave up on that idea and decided to make a cat that bounces off the edges (partly inspired by the DVD screensaver). To create the code, I used materials and knowledge from our classes and video tutorials from the freeCodeCamp and The Coding Train channels.


Multiples
My work is dedicated to recent events that took place in my home country. Qantar ’22 is called “Bloody January” in Kazakhstan , where protests happened over a sudden increase in liquefied gas prices which escalated into nationwide unrest in early 2022. In Almaty, demonstrators stormed the city hall and set it on fire, seized the airport, and disrupted cellular and internet services to prevent the coordination of the unrest. A state of emergency was declared in the country, and security forces were allowed to “shoot to kill.” A CSTO peacekeeping mission was sent to protect key facilities. “Bloody January” (the Kazakh word “Qantar” means January) will forever be remembered by Kazakhstani citizens. 
 The sun with 32 rays is a symbol from the Kazakhstani flag, and the building with the fire is the burning city hall. The colors were chosen to convey the tragic atmosphere of the events.

 
Media
On the topic of media, I wrote code where the number of notifications gradually increases, they start to take over the entire screen, and the notification sound becomes annoying to the user. With this, I wanted to show the digital overload people experience every day, since digitalization has become a constant part of our lives and is taking up more and more space.
